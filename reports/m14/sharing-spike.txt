What this cannot establish here
  The guest cannot MOUNT the share: this fixture's kernel has no 9p
  driver, built in or as a module, so nothing inside it can read the
  shared directory. Reading a shared file from a guest waits for an
  Android guest (Milestone 4) — and whether 9p is even the right
  transport for Android is itself open.

  Clipboard text cannot cross either: QEMU mediates between this
  process and a guest clipboard agent, and neither this fixture nor a
  stock Android image runs one.
